## About me

Hey, I'm Donnie D'Amato, a user experience architect who finds creativity within constraints, with a background spanning design, front-end engineering, and higher education. I've worked across the design systems practice for many years, from token architecture and governance to the strategic frameworks that bring coherence across an organization. Now I'm bringing that same discipline to a new frontier: shaping how AI behaves with the rigor we expect from a best in class interface.

## Problems I Enjoy Solving

- **Translating an organization's intent into reusable guidelines**, the kind that once shaped components and tokens, now shaping how AI behaves: its voice, tone, trust, and safety
- **Pioneering systems where including every user advances the business**; serving more people and growing it become the same winning move
- **Teaching the next generation** through writing, speaking, and the classroom; sharing the thinking openly so the whole practice matures faster
